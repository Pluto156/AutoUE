【Scene Description】
The game takes place in a small forest clearing. At the center of the scene stands a wooden cabin, with several interactive objects distributed around it:
Chest: Placed on the left side of the camp. It can be opened to obtain items.
Campfire: Located at the center of the camp. It can be lit or extinguished.
EnemyGuard: Patrols around the outer area of the camp and will enter a combat state when the player approaches.
The surrounding area contains non-interactive decorative elements such as trees and rocks. The player character can move freely, and interaction actions can be performed when approaching interactive objects.
【Gameplay Description】
The player’s objective is to complete a series of basic interaction tasks within the forest camp. The core gameplay includes:
Movement
The player can move freely to any interactive object in the scene.
Open Chest (Chest)
When the player approaches the chest, an interaction action can be performed to add the items inside the chest to the inventory (InventoryModule).
Light Campfire (Campfire)
When the player moves near the campfire and interacts with it, the campfire can be lit, triggering a small quest-related event (QuestModule).
