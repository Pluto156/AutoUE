# 02 结构化拆解：行为-能力-实体

分析顺序：行为 → 能力 → 实体；机器落档：entities → abilities → behaviors。

## Entity: `player` · Player
Owns player movement, short melee attack, dash, and temporary frozen state response.

### Ability: `player.horizontal_movement` · Horizontal Movement
Allows basic side-scroller movement when not frozen.
- Behavior `player.horizontal_movement.move_left_right`: When the player gives left or right movement input while not frozen → The player moves horizontally across the room floor. → The player can reposition within the compact combat room.

### Ability: `player.short_melee_attack` · Short Melee Attack
Allows the player to perform one close-range attack.
- Behavior `player.short_melee_attack.strike_enemy`: When the player performs the melee attack near the enemy → The player makes a short close-range strike that can hit the patrolling melee enemy. → A hit enemy loses health.

### Ability: `player.brief_dash` · Brief Dash
Allows a short burst of horizontal movement when not frozen.
- Behavior `player.brief_dash.dash_horizontally`: When the player dashes while not frozen → The player quickly moves a short distance in the current horizontal direction. → The player briefly repositions faster than normal movement.

### Ability: `player.frozen_state` · Frozen State
Temporarily blocks player movement after ice trap overlap.
- Behavior `player.frozen_state.become_frozen`: When the ice trap freezes the player → The player enters a short frozen state and horizontal movement is blocked. → The player visibly cannot move for a brief time.
- Behavior `player.frozen_state.thaw`: When the short frozen duration ends → The player leaves the frozen state and movement is restored. → The player can move and dash again.

## Entity: `patrolling_melee_enemy` · Patrolling Melee Enemy
Owns enemy patrol, health loss, close-range threat, and defeated state.

### Ability: `patrolling_melee_enemy.patrol` · Patrol
Moves the enemy back and forth inside the room.
- Behavior `patrolling_melee_enemy.patrol.move_back_and_forth`: When the enemy is active and not defeated → The enemy patrols along a short horizontal path in the room. → The enemy creates a moving melee hazard for the player.

### Ability: `patrolling_melee_enemy.melee_pressure` · Melee Pressure
Represents the enemy's close-range combat behavior.
- Behavior `patrolling_melee_enemy.melee_pressure.threaten_close_player`: When the active enemy is close to the player → The enemy presents a melee danger at close range. → The player must avoid or defeat the enemy in close combat.

### Ability: `patrolling_melee_enemy.health` · Health
Tracks enemy damage and defeat.
- Behavior `patrolling_melee_enemy.health.take_melee_damage`: When the player's short melee attack hits the enemy → The enemy loses health. → Repeated successful hits can reduce the enemy to defeat.
- Behavior `patrolling_melee_enemy.health.become_defeated`: When the enemy health reaches zero → The enemy enters its defeated state and stops acting as an active patrol threat. → The room exit becomes eligible to unlock.

## Entity: `ice_trap` · Ice Trap
Owns overlap-triggered freezing behavior.

### Ability: `ice_trap.freeze_trigger` · Freeze Trigger
Freezes the player when overlapped.
- Behavior `ice_trap.freeze_trigger.freeze_overlapping_player`: When the player overlaps the ice trap → The trap applies a short frozen state to the player and signals freeze feedback. → The player is temporarily immobilized, freeze VFX appears, and the side camera shakes slightly.

## Entity: `freeze_vfx` · Freeze VFX
Owns the visible freeze feedback shown when the player is trapped.

### Ability: `freeze_vfx.freeze_feedback` · Freeze Feedback
Displays a brief visual effect for the frozen state.
- Behavior `freeze_vfx.freeze_feedback.appear_on_freeze`: When the ice trap freezes the player → A visible freeze effect appears around or on the player for the frozen moment. → The player can see that the frozen state has been applied.

## Entity: `side_camera` · Side Camera
Owns small camera feedback when the ice trap freezes the player.

### Ability: `side_camera.freeze_feedback_shake` · Freeze Feedback Shake
Provides a small camera shake for trap feedback.
- Behavior `side_camera.freeze_feedback_shake.shake_on_freeze`: When the ice trap freezes the player → The side camera gives a small brief shake. → The trap hit feels noticeable without changing the one-room side-scroller view.

## Entity: `locked_exit` · Locked Exit
Owns exit lock state and room completion.

### Ability: `locked_exit.enemy_gate` · Enemy Gate
Keeps the exit locked until the enemy is defeated.
- Behavior `locked_exit.enemy_gate.remain_locked`: While the patrolling melee enemy is not defeated → The exit prevents room completion. → The player cannot finish the room before defeating the enemy.
- Behavior `locked_exit.enemy_gate.unlock_after_enemy_defeat`: When the patrolling melee enemy becomes defeated → The exit changes from locked to unlocked. → The player can now complete the room by reaching the exit.

### Ability: `locked_exit.room_completion` · Room Completion
Completes the room after the exit is unlocked and reached.
- Behavior `locked_exit.room_completion.complete_on_reach`: When the player reaches the unlocked exit → The room is marked complete. → The player completes the compact combat room.
